# Knowledge Base — Index

The growing catalogue of every strategy dossier. One row per strategy hypothesis.
The **corpus**, not any single strategy, is the asset: as rows accumulate, the last two
columns (meta-hypotheses, robust-vs-fragile traits) become the real output of the platform.

> ⚠️ Research only. No execution, no signals, no advice. A `verdict` here is a statement about
> *evidence*, never a recommendation to trade.

## Meta-hypotheses (STEP 7 — grow with the corpus)

| meta_id | statement | member strategies | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M-001** | Lagging trend/crossover rules show low OOS robustness once search cost is priced in. | H-001, H-010, H-012, H-013, H-015, H-022 | 5/6 tested — 5 support / 0 contradict |
| **M-002** | Simple price-action momentum rules do not survive costs/turnover. | H-002, H-006, H-007, H-011 | 4/4 tested — 4 support / 0 contradict |
| **M-003** | Mean-reversion oscillator/band rules are strongly regime-dependent. | H-003, H-004, H-009, H-020, H-021 | 5/5 tested — 5 support / 0 contradict |
| **M-004** | Breakout/volatility rules concentrate their edge in **trending** regimes (refined 2026-07-08: on 5 assets the edge was trend-concentrated, **not** crisis-concentrated — crisis Sharpe ≈ 0). | H-005, H-018, H-019, H-024 | 4/4 tested — 4 support / 0 contradict |
| **M-005** | Bar-pattern/pivot rules have too few, too-noisy signals for a credible edge. | H-008, H-014, H-016, H-017 | 4/4 tested — 4 support / 0 contradict |
| **M-006** | Composite signals do not beat their best single component after correction. | H-023 | 1/1 tested — 1 support / 0 contradict |
| **M-007 (NEW)** | Momentum (trend/breakout) and mean-reversion rules have **opposite, near-symmetric regime signatures**; neither is unconditionally robust, but each carries a conditional regime-gated edge. | H-005, H-010, H-020 (+future) | seeded 2026-07-08 — supported by the 5-asset study |

*A meta-hypothesis is only as strong as the number of independent strategies bearing on it. First
evidence is in: see [`CROSS_ASSET_GENERALIZATION.md`](../CROSS_ASSET_GENERALIZATION.md). Regime
dependence (M-003, M-007) is the single largest effect observed so far.*

## Robust-vs-fragile trait ledger (STEP 7 — populated as verdicts land)

Once ≥ 2 strategies reach a verdict, this table starts separating the traits that travel with
robustness from those that travel with fragility. Empty by design until real evidence exists.

| trait | robust strategies (n) | fragile strategies (n) | direction of association |
|---|---|---|---|
| free-parameter count | — | H-002 (0 params) | fewer params did NOT help; the null has none |
| turnover | — | H-002 (very high), H-010 | high turnover → decisively negative (H-002 worst) |
| regime-dependence | — | H-005, H-010, H-020 (all extreme) | **strongest signal so far:** every tested rule is regime-gated; unconditional edge = none |
| cost-sensitivity | — | all four | edges shrink across the {0,5,10} bps ladder |
| out-of-sample degradation | — | all four | in-sample → OOS retention low; none Supported OOS |

*First four strategies all landed fragile, so the "robust" column is empty by design — a full
Graveyard here is the instrument working, not failing. The trait that most separates behaviour is
**regime-dependence** (see M-003, M-007).*
